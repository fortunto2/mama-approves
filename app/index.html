How it works

  1. Your photo goes to Jev, TypeSafe AI's decision model, as the state. One request.
  2. Nine typed questions ride along: home-made? vegetables? warm meal? junk? portion? expired? allergen? category? food at all?
  3. Jev answers each with a calibrated probability in one parallel pass. It never generates text, so there is nothing to parse and nothing to hallucinate.
  4. Mom's rules are weights in code (home-made 0.25, vegetables 0.25, no junk 0.20, warm 0.15, portion 0.15). Change a number, not a prompt.
  5. Confidence below 0.6, or no food in frame → Show me closer instead of a guess. Expired > 0.5 caps the score at 0.1.

Latency 0.3–1.5 s on a 768 px photo, all nine answers in one call. No LLM anywhere in the loop.
